Mithra's feast

The one day the King could get drunk

Greek writers knew a Persian feast of Mithra, the Mithrakana. It is the best-attested Iranian feast of the Achaemenid age, though not from a Persian text.

게임에서: Mithra's feast comes in the autumn. Wine and meat make it grand.

A drunk king

고대의 전승

Ctesias and Duris, quoted by Athenaeus, say that on Mithra's feast alone the Persian king may get drunk and dance the Persian dance. Ctesias was a doctor at Artaxerxes II's court, so this is about a century after our levels.

Athenaeus 10.434e

Twenty thousand foals

고대의 전승

Strabo says the satrap of Armenia sent the King 20,000 foals each year for the Mithrakana. That is a large number from a late source, but the name is there.

Strabo 11.14.9

Which month

당대 기록

Iranica's article suggests the name Mehragan came in with the calendar reform of the later fifth century. In Darius's own calendar the autumn month is Bagayadi, "of the worship of the god", and which god is itself argued. The game's date falls in that month.

Iranica, "Mehragan"; Behistun, DB
