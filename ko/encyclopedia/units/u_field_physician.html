Field Physician

Army doctors of the Persian age, 6th-5th centuries BCE

Armies of this age carried physicians for the wounded, though few sources name them. The Persian court prized foreign doctors, above all Egyptians and then Greeks. Xenophon, marching home with the Ten Thousand, says how the Greeks chose eight men as doctors because of the many wounded.

Eight doctors for the wounded

고대의 전승

Retreating through Mesopotamia with their wounded, the Greek Ten Thousand halted for three days and appointed eight men as doctors, because there were so many hurt. Xenophon does not say how much these men knew. What mattered was that someone was named to do the work.

Xenophon, Anabasis 3.4.30

The King's foreign doctors

고대의 전승

Herodotus says Cyrus asked Egypt's king for the best eye doctor there, and that Darius kept Egyptian physicians at court. When they failed with his twisted ankle, the Greek Democedes, a slave among the prisoners, cured him and was made rich.

Herodotus 3.1, 3.129-130

A doctor on the battlefield

고대의 전승

Ctesias, a Greek physician at the court of Artaxerxes II, was in the field at Cunaxa in 401 BCE and said he treated the King's wound himself. Xenophon cites him for this, so at least one royal doctor rode with the army.

Xenophon, Anabasis 1.8.26
