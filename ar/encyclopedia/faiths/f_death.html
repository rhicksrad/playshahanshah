Exposure and burial

Birds, dogs and wax

Herodotus says the magi leave their dead to birds and dogs before burial, and other Persians coat the body in wax and bury it. Kings had tombs. The towers of silence are much later.

في اللعبة: The Burial Ground takes the city's dead, and unburied dead sicken those near them.

What Herodotus was told

رواية قديمة

He says it was told to him as a secret and that he isn't sure, except of the magi, who do it openly. Bodies are torn by a bird or dog before the wax and the earth.

Herodotus 1.140

Kings' tombs

في السجلات

Cyrus lay on a golden couch inside a stone chamber at Pasargadae. Darius and his heirs cut their tombs into the cliff at Naqsh-e Rostam. Neither is exposure, and both were sealed against the earth.

Arrian, Anabasis 6.29; Naqsh-e Rostam

Towers of silence

متنازع عليه

The towers known from later Zoroastrian practice have no secure Achaemenid example. Rock-cut niches and ossuaries of doubtful date are argued over.

Boyce 1982; Briant 2002
