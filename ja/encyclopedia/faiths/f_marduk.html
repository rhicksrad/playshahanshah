Marduk and the Akitu

Babylon's god under Persian kings

ゲームでは： レベル1：建国者の墓

The Cyrus Cylinder has Marduk himself choose Cyrus. His priests wrote it. It's the best evidence for how an old temple made its peace with a new king, and the worst for what Cyrus believed.

ゲームでは： Babylonian brickmakers at Susa want a Shrine of Marduk and the Akitu. Marduk's table is the rite that lifts them.

Chosen by Marduk

同時代の記録

The Cylinder says Marduk searched every land for a just king and took Cyrus by the hand. It says Cyrus returned displaced gods to their shrines. Written for Babylonian eyes in a Babylonian form, it says nothing about Persian religion and is no charter of rights.

Cyrus Cylinder (BM 90920) 11-12, 32

New year in Nisannu

同時代の記録

The Akitu ran the first twelve days of Nisannu, spring. Marduk's statue went in procession to the Akitu house outside the city. The Nabonidus Chronicle complains year after year that it lapsed while the king was away.

Nabonidus Chronicle; Kuhrt 2007

Did Persian kings take his hand?

係争中

The Chronicle has Cambyses in Babylon at the new year of 538, and older books made that an Akitu. Kuhrt and Sherwin-White read it as an installation, and find no secure Persian-period Akitu with the king present. Their same paper argues that the story of Xerxes destroying Babylon's temples is Greek, and Boiy calls it fiction.

Nabonidus Chronicle iii 24-28; Kuhrt and Sherwin-White 1987; Boiy 2004
