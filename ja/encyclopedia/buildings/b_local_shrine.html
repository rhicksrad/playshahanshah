Shrines of the peoples

The King's other gods

ゲームでは： レベル1：建国者の墓

The empire held many peoples and paid for many gods. A town's second people wants its own priests and its own feasts, and the King's officials knew it.

ゲームでは： A level's own shrine serves its second people: Humban at Parsa, Marduk at Susa, Hera on the Ister, Apollo at Marathon, a shrine at Acanthus at Athos.

Humban at Parsa

同時代の記録

Fortification tablets issue grain and wine for Humban and other Elamite gods, on the same terms as Ahuramazda.

Henkelman 2008

Marduk at Susa

同時代の記録

Darius's charter says Babylonians made Susa's bricks. Their god's shrine there is the game's reasonable guess, not an excavated building.

DSf; Cyrus Cylinder

Greek shrines

古代の記述

Mandrocles dedicated to Hera after the Bosporus bridge, and Datis to Apollo on Delos. The Ionians came with their gods, though what they built on campaign is unknown, so these shrines are guesses too.

Herodotus 4.88, 6.97
