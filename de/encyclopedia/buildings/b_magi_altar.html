Altar of the Magi

An open altar for the Persians of another people's town

Im Spiel: Level 1: Das Grab des Gründers

Where the town's main temple is someone else's, the Persians still need a fire and a magus. Two plinths and a stepped altar in the open air, after Pasargadae and the tomb reliefs, is as much as the evidence allows.

Im Spiel: Built where another people's temple is the main one (Memphis, Susa, Sardis, Plataea). Its magus walks to the Persian quarters, and the Persian rites can be held here.

The plinths at Pasargadae

Zeitgenössisch belegt

Two limestone plinths stand in the sacred precinct at Pasargadae, one with a stair. What stood on them is inferred from later reliefs: the king, a fire, or both.

Stronach 1978

The altar on the tomb

Zeitgenössisch belegt

Above Darius's tomb the King stands on a stepped base before a burning altar, a winged figure above. It's the model for this altar's shape.

Naqsh-e Rostam, tomb of Darius; Garrison 2017

Why no temple

Umstritten

No Achaemenid fire temple is secure. So the Altar has no roof and no door.

Iranica, "Fire altars"; Herodotus 1.131
