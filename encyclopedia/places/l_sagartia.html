Sagartia

Land of the Sagartian nomads of Iran, which rose under Citrantakhma in 521 BCE

In the game: Level 3: The Lie

Sagartia was the land of the Sagartians, Iranian nomads of the plateau east of Media. In 521 a Sagartian named Citrantakhma claimed to be king of Sagartia of the line of Cyaxares and rose against Darius. Herodotus knows the Sagartians as horsemen who fought with the lasso.

In the game: Sagartia is named in the Bagastana level's Chronicle and the King's generals' campaigns, among the lands that rose in 522-521.

Citrantakhma's rising

In the record

Darius says a Sagartian named Citrantakhma rebelled, saying he was king in Sagartia of the family of Cyaxares. Darius sent against him a Mede, Takhmaspada, who beat him and brought him in. Darius cut off his nose and ears, and impaled him at Arbela.

Behistun inscription §33

Riders with the lasso

Ancient account

Herodotus says the Sagartians were nomads, Persian in race and speech, who brought eight thousand horse to Xerxes' army. They carried no weapons of bronze or iron but daggers, and fought by throwing ropes of plaited leather with nooses, dragging down horse or man.

Herodotus 7.85

A Persian tribe

Ancient account

Herodotus lists the Sagartians among the tribes of the Persians, with the Daans, Mardians and others who were nomads, as distinct from the farming tribes. For tribute they were put in the fourteenth division with the Sarangians and others, paying six hundred talents.

Herodotus 1.125, 3.93

Where was Sagartia?

Disputed

Its place is uncertain. Most historians put it on the central plateau east of Media, perhaps toward modern Yazd, and see it as a pastoral land rather than a province with cities. The Sagartians' link to the Median royal line in Citrantakhma's claim is also unexplained.

P. Briant, From Cyrus to Alexander, 2002
